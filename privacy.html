1. Who we are

IAS Florida LLC, a Florida limited liability company, operates iasflorida.com and is the controller of the information described here. We serve Miami-Dade County and surrounding Florida communities.

This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and exactly how to get it deleted.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact details — name, phone number, email address
  • Service details — property ZIP code or address, the service you need, urgency, and anything you write in the message field
  • Appointment details — the date and time you select when booking
  • Job records — what we diagnosed, what we did, parts installed, and warranty dates
  • Membership details — plan selected and billing status, if you enroll

Information collected automatically

  • Pages viewed, how you reached the site, approximate region (city level, from IP), device and browser type
  • A stored preference for your chosen language, and whether you accepted analytics cookies

What we do not collect through this website: payment card numbers, bank details, Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, or any government identification number. Our forms never ask for these. Never send them through a web form. Card payments are taken in person by the technician at the time of service.

3. How we use it

  • To contact you about the service you requested
  • To schedule, perform, and follow up on work
  • To prepare estimates, invoices, and warranty records
  • To administer membership plans and billing
  • To answer questions and resolve complaints
  • To understand which parts of our site are useful, if you accepted analytics
  • To meet legal, licensing, insurance, warranty, and tax obligations

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

4. What we never do

  • We do not sell your personal information. Not to data brokers, not to lead aggregators, not to anyone.
  • We do not rent, trade, or share it with third parties for their own marketing.
  • We do not share your service history with other contractors.

5. Who we share with

Only with service providers that help us operate, and only what each one needs:

  • Form processing — submissions from our estimate and contact forms are delivered through a third-party form service that passes them to our email
  • Appointment scheduling — bookings are handled by a third-party scheduling provider that stores the details needed to confirm your appointment
  • Website analytics — Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies
  • Website hosting — our site is served by a third-party hosting platform which receives standard server request data
  • Payment processing — handled at the time of service by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers
  • Professional advisers — accountants, insurers, or attorneys, where necessary and confidential

We may also disclose information when required by law, subpoena, or regulatory authority, or where necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of our customers, our team, or the public. In the event of a sale or merger of the business, customer records may transfer to the successor, who would remain bound by this policy.

6. Cookies and analytics

Our site uses a small amount of browser storage to remember your language choice and your cookie preference. These are essential to how the site works and are not used for tracking.

Analytics are off by default. Google Analytics does not load unless you click "Accept all" on our cookie banner. If you choose "Essential only," no analytics cookies are set at all.

To change your mind later, clear your browser's site data for iasflorida.com and the banner will reappear. You can also install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on, or use your browser's Do Not Track and cookie-blocking settings. The site works normally either way.

7. How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as we have a reason to:

  • Form submissions that do not become jobs — [e.g. 12 months], then deleted
  • Customer and job records — [e.g. 4 years] after the last service, to support warranty claims and service history
  • Warranty documentation — for the length of the applicable warranty plus [e.g. 1 year]
  • Invoices, payment records, and tax documents — [e.g. 7 years], as accounting and tax rules require
  • Membership billing records — [e.g. 7 years] after cancellation
  • Analytics data — retained according to our analytics provider's settings, currently [e.g. 14 months]
  • Email correspondence — [e.g. 3 years]

When a retention period ends, we delete the information or irreversibly anonymize it.

Why some records outlast a deletion request: Florida law, our insurance, and tax rules require us to keep certain job and financial records for a set period. If you ask us to delete everything, we will delete what we can and tell you specifically what we must keep, why, and when it will be destroyed.

8. Your rights

We extend the following rights to every customer, regardless of where you live:

  • Access — ask what information we hold about you and get a copy
  • Correction — ask us to fix anything inaccurate or incomplete
  • Deletion — ask us to erase your information, subject to records we are legally required to keep (see section 9)
  • Portability — receive your information in a common, machine-readable format
  • Restriction — ask us to limit how we use your information while a dispute is resolved
  • Objection — object to a particular use of your information
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw analytics consent at any time, without affecting anything done before
  • Opt out of contact — tell us to stop contacting you, other than what is needed to complete work already underway

Exercising any of these rights is free, and we will never treat you differently for doing so.

9. How to delete your data

Here is the exact process.

Step 1 — Send the request

Email jobs@iasflorida.com with the subject line "Data deletion request", or call (786) 372-7744 and say you want your data deleted. Include the name, phone number, and email address you used with us so we can find your records.

Step 2 — We verify it is you

We will confirm your identity before deleting anything — usually by contacting you at the phone number or email already on file. This protects you: without verification, anyone could erase your warranty records.

Step 3 — We respond within 30 days

We will confirm in writing within 30 days of verifying your identity. If the request is unusually complex we may need up to [30 additional] days, and we will tell you before that happens.

Step 4 — What gets deleted

We delete your contact details, service inquiries, appointment history, marketing records, and analytics identifiers from our active systems, and instruct our form and scheduling providers to do the same.

What we may have to keep, and why

  • Invoices and financial records — required for tax and accounting compliance
  • Warranty records — needed to honor an active warranty; if you delete these, we may be unable to honor a future claim, and we will tell you before proceeding
  • Records subject to an active dispute, claim, or legal hold
  • Permit and licensing records — where the county or state requires retention

Anything we retain is locked down to that purpose only — not used for marketing, not used for anything else — and destroyed when its retention period expires.

Backups. Deleted data may persist in encrypted backups for up to [e.g. 90 days] before those backups cycle out. It is not accessible or used during that time.

10. Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards: our site is served over HTTPS, access to customer records is limited to team members who need it, and we do not store payment card numbers.

No method of transmitting data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. That is another reason our forms never ask for sensitive identifiers. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as Florida law requires.

11. Text messages

When you give us your phone number, you consent to receive calls and texts about your service request and appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out of texts. We do not share phone numbers with third parties for their marketing.

12. Children

Our services are directed to adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

13. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top. Material changes will be noted prominently on this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
