Payment

How you pay,
spelled out.

No surprises at the end of the job. Here's exactly how billing works and what we accept.

Credit & debit

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Charged on-site after the work is done, never before.

Cash & check

Accepted on completion. You always get a written, itemized receipt — ask and it's yours before we leave.

Financing

Monthly payment plans on new system installations. Application handled on-site, most decisions same visit.

Membership billing

Deep Freeze members are billed monthly on the card on file. Cancel anytime — no contract, no cancellation fee.

How billing works

  • Written estimate before any work begins — you approve the price first
  • Diagnostic fee is quoted on the phone, before we dispatch — never a surprise
  • If the scope changes mid-job, we stop and get your approval before continuing
  • No payment collected before work is completed on standard service calls
  • Larger installations may require a deposit, stated in writing on the estimate
